# Privacy Policy — Muse for Windows

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-25  
**Developer:** 1MS Studio (`1RS v1.2`)

---

## Summary

- **Zero Telemetry:** Muse for Windows collects **no** analytics, **no** crash telemetry, **no** keystroke/clipboard monitoring, and **no** conversation logs.
- **100% Local Desktop Storage:** Settings, window bounds, permission policies, and sanitized SQLite audit logs remain strictly on your Windows PC unless you explicitly export them.
- **Official Muse Service (`https://muse.ai`):** When using Mode A, your authentication session, conversations, Feed, Ideas, Goals, Library, and Cloud Computer are processed directly by **Meta Platforms, Inc.** under official Muse terms and privacy policies inside the isolated `persist:muse-official` Chromium session partition.

For complete details on local storage paths, DPAPI encryption, and data deletion, read [`docs/PRIVACY.md`](./docs/PRIVACY.md).
