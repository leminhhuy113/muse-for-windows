## 2. Original Brand Mark Geometry

To ensure zero infringement on Meta Platforms, Inc. or third-party trademarks, **Muse for Windows** uses a 100% original geometric mark designed by **1MS Studio**:
- **Outer Squircle Frame:** Carbon `#0B0D10` rounded precision tile (`rx=22%`) with subtle `#282D36` bevel stroke, ensuring high contrast on both dark and light Windows 11 taskbars.
- **Monogram Emblem:** Geometric twin-pillar **"M"** fused with an ascending **1MS Studio spark node** rendered in `#FAFF69` Electric Lime, engineered to remain razor-sharp down to `16×16` System Tray and Taskbar pixels.
